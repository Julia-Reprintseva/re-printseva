SafeOut —
the bot that won't forget

A Telegram safety bot for dates with people you only know online. It stores all the meeting details and alerts your trusted contacts on its own if you go quiet.

RU · EN · TR languages Free · non-commercial August 2026
↗ Open the bot in Telegram Full instructions →
Why this exists

When a message to a friend is not enough

You can send a friend the guy's details and ask them to call if you haven't texted by midnight. But your friend might get distracted, fall asleep, or simply miss it. And you have to remember to text "all good" yourself.

SafeOut doesn't forget. You tap "Start date" — 30 minutes later the bot asks "how are you?". You don't reply — 15 minutes after that it texts your friend itself, without any action from you. You physically can't respond — the bot fires anyway.

Step 01

Quick start

  1. Open @safeout_dates_bot and press Start.
  2. Choose your language (RU / EN / TR).
  3. Add at least one trusted contact — /contacts → Add contact.
Important about contacts. Telegram doesn't allow a bot to message people who have never launched it. After you add a contact, the bot sends you an invite link — forward it to your friend. They follow the link and press Start. Until they do, their name shows "⏳ waiting to connect" and the bot can't reach them in an emergency.
Step 02

Before the date — /newdate

The bot will ask one by one:

  • the person's name and their handle on the dating app;
  • a link to their profile (Tinder, Instagram…);
  • where you're meeting and where you might go next;
  • car plate and description;
  • any other important info;
  • the time you plan to be home.

Everything optional can be skipped with /skip. Then attach photos, chat screenshots, document photos, voice messages. When done — ✅ Done with files.

Where do the files go. Nowhere — when you send a file it stays on Telegram's servers, like any message to a bot. SafeOut only saves the reference, not the bytes. The file becomes accessible only when an alert fires — and only for someone who has the alert page link. That link has no password, so don't share it with strangers.
Step 03

Safety checklist

The bot shows a 19-item checklist — it auto-checks some items from your answers, the rest you confirm manually. Items marked (!) are mandatory safety basics (first meeting in a public place, etc.) and block the start until checked. When ready — ▶️ All set — start the date.

Step 04

During the date

Right after starting, share your live location (paperclip → Location → Share Live Location) — this way contacts will see where you are on the alert page.

Every 15 minutes the bot sends three quick questions with buttons:

  • All good — next check in 15 minutes;
  • Something's off — the bot offers a full checklist, calling a contact, or noting it and continuing;
  • SOS — immediate alert.

If you don't respond — 15 minutes later the bot automatically messages connected contacts with all saved details and a link to the alert page.

Step 05

SOS and getting home

The SOS button is in every bot message during the date — contacts get alerted immediately, no 15-minute wait.

When you're home: tap I'm home, all good — the bot closes the date and messages contacts that everything is fine.

For trusted contacts

If someone sent you an invite link

Follow the link and press Start. From that moment the bot can message you if your person has a problem on a date. The alert message contains a link to a page with all the details: who, where, the car, photos, last known location.
