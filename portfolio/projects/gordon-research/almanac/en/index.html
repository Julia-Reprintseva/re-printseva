The “Digital YuG” project · work on the book begins

How the book got going again

By early 2025 the book had stalled. I suggested preserving the chapters and bringing AI into the work, and on 18 June 2025 we launched the “Digital YuG” project together: I used neural networks to dig up material for the book “Letters of the 20th Century”. On launch day the author wrote:

“Today the project ‘Digital I——oG’ starts, in which the tamer of neurosaurs Julia Reprintseva will create from my thoughts and words either an assistant or even a double, who is to cope with mining material for a stubborn book.”

“Once Julia’s and my project is ready, I dream of finishing the book by New Year.”

Yuri Gordon, Facebook* post, 18 June 2025
