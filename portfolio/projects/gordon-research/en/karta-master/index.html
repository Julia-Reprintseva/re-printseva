How the AI led Bezdomny around Moscow

For Yuri Gordon’s map “The Master and Moscow” I gathered Bulgakov’s and Mandelstam’s Moscow topography with AI — and then checked it. Here are three layers of that check on one schematic: the chase after Woland, the novel’s addresses and Mandelstam’s Moscow. Gold is what was confirmed, red is what the AI invented or got wrong. Hover over a point to open a card with the quotation. This map exists nowhere else: I built it for the portfolio from the research materials and checked every quotation again.

What the AI claimed — and what turned out to be true

Each card is one mistake from the research and the fact-check. Struck through is the AI’s version; below is what checking against the text and sources showed.

The route and Bulgakov’s addresses

The route of the chaseIvan “first runs along Bolshaya Sadovaya” and “down Tverskaya to the Central Telegraph”In the novel: Patriarshy Lane → Spiridonovka → Nikitsky Gates → Arbat Square → Kropotkin Street → Ostozhenka → the Moskva river. No Sadovaya, no Tverskaya.“The Master and Margarita”, ch. 4; Yuri Gordon’s remark
Griboyedov House“Griboyedov House (Prechistenka St.)”Herzen House, Tverskoy Boulevard, 25. In the novel the house stands “on the boulevard ring” — the text itself rules out Prechistenka.“The Master and Margarita”, ch. 5
The Variety Theatre“The Variety Theatre” on BersenevskayaThe Music Hall at Bolshaya Sadovaya, 18 — the office windows “looked out on Sadovaya”. The Variety Theatre on Bersenevskaya misses the mark.“The Master and Margarita”, ch. 10; bulgakov.ru
The jump into the river“Prechistenskaya Embankment opposite Savvinsky Lane”Opposite Pozharsky (then Savelyevsky) Lane — as the very source cited by the fact-check says.in-moskow.livejournal.com/3098
Nashchokinsky Lane“His last home… (1936–1940)”From 1934. Further down, the same report says Bulgakov and Mandelstam both lived in this house in 1934.Bulgakov’s biography
Alexander GardenMargarita meets Azazello — “chapter 20”Chapter 19, “Margarita”: “sitting under the Kremlin wall on one of the benches”.“The Master and Margarita”, ch. 19

Mandelstam’s Moscow: lines that don’t exist

Invented by AI“A woman from Khamovniki, timid”No such line — “Khamovniki” never occurs in Mandelstam.Collected works, rvb.ru
Invented by AI“I’m at the Metropol, and you’re on the Arbat”No. The real Metropol is in “Cold Summer” (1923): “a world hotel”.Collected works, rvb.ru
Invented by AI“by the Lubyanka a street lamp burnt out”There is no such line.Collected works, rvb.ru
Invented by AI“at Chistye Prudy”Chistye Prudy never occurs in Mandelstam.Collected works, rvb.ru
Invented by AI“in the backwater of Zamoskvorechye, among the ruins of bathhouses”Not in “The Egyptian Stamp”. There is “a chance guest of Zamoskvorechye” — “Journey to Armenia”.Collected works, rvb.ru
Invented by AI“…to Moscow University and back”There is no such line.Collected works, rvb.ru
Invented by AI“boulevards-propylaea, like empty palaces”No. There is “the rustle of the boulevard propylaeum” — an early version of “Moscow Drizzle” (1922).Collected works, rvb.ru
Invented by AI“The skyline — without towers”There is no such line.Collected works, rvb.ru
Invented by AI“let’s go round the ring, as always”No. There is “I will run three times round the boulevard rings of Moscow” — “Fourth Prose”.Collected works, rvb.ru
Someone else’s line“gingerbread towers” — Mandelstam, draftsThis is Bulgakov: “the gingerbread towers of the Novodevichy Convent”, “The Master and Margarita”, ch. 32.“The Master and Margarita”, ch. 32
Wrong date“Where the bathing huts, the spinning mills…” — 1929May 1932. The poem is about Gorky Park and the Neskuchny Garden: “Dull-and-not-dull hills, like halva”.Collected works, rvb.ru
