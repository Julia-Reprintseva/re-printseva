AI report · “The Master and Moscow” fact-check

Ivan Bezdomny’s route

The route of the chase: Patriarch’s Ponds → Spiridonovka → Nikitsky Gates → Arbat Square, then the Arbat lanes, Kropotkin Street and Ostozhenka.

The place of the jump into the river is Prechistenskaya Embankment opposite Savvinsky Lane.

Conclusion: the route fully corresponds to historical Moscow. No errors.

Bulgakov’s addresses

The “Bad Apartment” — Bolshaya Sadovaya, 10, apt. 50. Griboyedov House (Prechistenka St.).

Nashchokinsky Lane, 3/5 — his last home, with Elena Shilovskaya (1936–1940). Malaya Nikitskaya, 3 — his address in the years of greatest creative activity.

At the mirrored doors of the Torgsin on the Smolensky market — chapter 28.

Mandelstam’s Moscow

Mandelstam jumped from a hospital window during his exile in Voronezh; Bulgakov witnessed the arrest.

“And the unruly grain trade at Sukharevka” — a poem of 1918. “A woman from Khamovniki, timid” — poems of the 1920s.

“The rings of the boulevards revel in black smallpox” — 1931. “Gingerbread towers” — Mandelstam’s drafts. “By the Lubyanka a street lamp burnt out”.
