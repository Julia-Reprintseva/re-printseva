WHY ME

From journalism to producing AI projects

Years in journalism and media taught me one rule: before you publish anything — check it. Since 2025 I've been applying that rule to AI: teaching people and teams to use it deliberately, instead of blindly trusting the first answer.

I started as an editor and journalist at national outlets, led editorial teams, and launched special projects. Now — private practice: an original course for people starting from zero, corporate training for teams, and custom AI assistants built for specific tasks.
