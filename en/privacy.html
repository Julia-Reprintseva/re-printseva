Privacy Policy

How data collection works on this site, and what it's used for.

What data is collected

This site uses web analytics services — Yandex.Metrica, Google Analytics, and Microsoft Clarity — to understand how many people visit the site, from which pages and devices, and how easy it is to use. These services automatically collect anonymized technical data: device and browser type, approximate location (city), traffic source, and on-page actions (clicks, scrolling, time on page).

The site does not request or store your name, contact details, or message unless you submit them yourself through the inquiry form or the contact buttons (Telegram, email) — in that case, the data is sent directly to the form service (Formspree) or to the relevant messenger/email service, outside this site's control.

Why this is needed

The data is used only to understand which parts of the site work well and which need improvement. Data is not sold or shared with third parties, other than the analytics and form services themselves (Yandex, Google, Microsoft, Formspree), which process it under their own privacy policies.

Cookies

The analytics services use cookies to recognize repeat visits. You can disable cookies in your browser settings — this won't prevent you from using the site, but analytics will stop counting you.
